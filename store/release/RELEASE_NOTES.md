# Heart Rate FX 1.0

First release.

- Sweeping ECG-style trace, one P-QRS-T complex per beat
- Beat timing taken from the watch's measured intervals where it reports them, with the
  averaged rate as a fallback
- Monitor beep at 880 Hz over a held-open stream, so the amplifier does not click between beats
- Backlight pulsing with the beat, colour and resting brightness adjustable
- Vibration click per beat
- Settings on the phone: sound, pitch, volume, vibration, backlight, trace speed and colour,
  language, demo pulse
- Nine languages on the watch: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese,
  Polish and Swedish
